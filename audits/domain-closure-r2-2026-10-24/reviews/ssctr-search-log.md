## Counterevidence and nonfindings

Concurrent training often retains maximal-strength/hypertrophy benefits; overlap is not proof of acute interference. Explosive strength is a distinct endpoint. Strength gains can persist during short interruptions; endurance losses vary. Taper evidence mainly studies trained endurance populations and cannot silently erase strength. RPE/talk test offer practical self-regulation with uncertainty, not interchangeable physiological thresholds. No searched source establishes that one synthetic low-dose recipe is universally safe, sufficient or optimal. No directly applicable universal pause-to-dose equation was found; this nonfinding is not proof that none exists.

Initial PMC anti-bot pages were not read as articles. Publisher alternatives are recorded above. Commercial/blog/social results, including Reddit, were excluded as policy authorities. No patient clearance, clinical screening, real-person efficacy, Product Truth promotion or runtime authorization is claimed by this source log.
