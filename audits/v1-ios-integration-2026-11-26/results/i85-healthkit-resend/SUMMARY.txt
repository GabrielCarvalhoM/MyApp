WHAT CHANGED
1. Re-send rounds (new HealthKitIngestor+Resend.swift, 99 lines). An item set aside ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED by an exhausted retryLater gets
   QueueItem.resendDue. deliveryConditionsChanged() and start() reopen it once due (state -> pending, still frozen), so buildDelivery
   puts the exact frozen bytes (same activityId, link, facts) in a NEW delivery (new deliveryId). A signal that comes before the round
   is due arms an in-memory wake for it (a launch arms it again). Triggers other than signal and launch (observer, reads) never reopen.
   Bound: Configuration.resend = ResendPolicy(maxRounds 8, base 900 s, cap 86 400 s): waits 15 min, 30 min, 1 h ... 24 h; each round is
   at most maxConsecutiveRetryLater sends; after the last round the item stays set aside for good. A due instant further away than the
   cap (clock went back) counts as due now (still bounded by the rounds).
2. Double-count guarantees of #48 kept. While a round is to come the item keeps its same-run group (regroup: `resendDue != nil`), so its
   copies stay consolidated and are never sent with another activityId. mayBeOnRecord is cleared ONLY by an answered NOT_RECORDED (on
   record, the same id and facts would be DUPLICATE). A refusal of the re-send (e.g. setAsideItem) records nothing NOW but proves nothing
   about the earlier send: the item stays may-be-on-record and the copies stay consolidated. A workout deleted in Apple Health is never
   sent again (G-5): merge clears its round, and a reopened-but-unsent one goes back to set aside; DELETED_MAYBE_ON_RECORD still once.
3. Over-caution QRE noted (InFlight.mayHaveLanded, not stored): a streak made only of refusals with the retryLater disposition
   (contract: NOTHING recorded) no longer makes the item may-be-on-record. Fail closed: a plain retryLater ("nothing was confirmed"),
   an answer B could not write, and any delivery read back from disk (a process that died may have sent it) all count as possibly landed.
   Note: CoachingHost (HostDeviceSink.swift:76) maps BUSY/PERSISTENCE_FAILURE/OPERATIONAL_ERROR to .retryLater(code:), never to
   .refused(_, _, .retryLater), so in production every exhausted streak is still may-be-on-record; the relaxation applies to the contract
   case only. The re-send (item 1) is what recovers the run in production.
4. Queue files written before #85 (no `resends` key): a may-be-on-record ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED item that was not deleted gets its rounds, the
   first due at once (a pre-#48 file may hold an item exhausted by NOT_RECORDED there; re-sending it is safe: DUPLICATE or decided anew).
5. Log: hki.resendReopened reopened=<n> (counts only). RetryScheduler/TaskRetryScheduler moved to +Resend; HealthKitIngestor.swift
   534 -> 528 (not grown). Every new or changed file <= 350.
