# G01–G04 Review Preparation — 0.2

Purpose: give real responsible professionals a bounded submission they can accept/reject/limit. This file and LLM reviews do not grant their approval. Existing authority is PDR §5 (G01–G04); no new registry/gate. Use the14 additive fixture prompts and source ledger, plus original r4 cases. Full population coverage is not presumed.

## Submission protocol shared by the four gaps

Review one rule/recipe/metadata subset at a time. Record: artifact/hash/revision; reviewer identity, qualification and scope; population/goals/equipment/task supported and excluded; applicable evidence/limitations; input/protocol/freshness/comparability; exact dose/units/envelope; essential stimulus; adequacy/necessity/Change Cost reasoning; KEEP and smaller-alternative accept/reject reasons; joint horizon; missing/conflicting-input behavior; agency/safety/reassessment; positive and negative witnesses; disposition ACCEPT/CONDITIONAL/REJECT/OUT_OF_SCOPE, rationale, conditions and withdrawal/validity. Signature/approval refs remain null until supplied by the actual authority.

Synthetic athleteA is an adult recreational review context, not an approved V1 population. Its running/strength history is incomplete synthetic reporting. Every numerical example is a question about representation and policy, not a suggested program. A reviewer can replace/reject a dose without changing historical r4 expectations.

## G01 — actual supported scope and repertoire

Owner: Founder allocates a real qualified physical-education/training professional and resources; legal service-model qualification remains as in PDR. Required decision: versioned adult profiles, Running objectives/session families, Strength repertoire/equipment/progression and restrictions/exclusions, with positive/negative examples.

Inspect32 concepts, seven example configurations,16 run families plus taper template. Decide whether the sample spans the intended novice/intermediate goals; no elite/clinical coverage inferred. Check calf/soleus, hamstring, unilateral and trunk support versus missing lateral-hip/adductor, carry/plyometric examples when material. Decide unresolved equipment AND/OR, laterality, muscle hierarchy/roles and pattern/mechanic vocabulary. Tags of intended running support do not prove transfer or injury prevention.

Positive review prompt: can the chosen barbell squat configuration be represented/covered for the declared adult profile and available rack? Negative prompt: family requires unresolved machine/support, unsupported profile or restriction; it is not eligible just because its name exists. Evidence of source permission/factual mapping remains separate from professional exercise coverage.

## G02 — dose, instruments, substitution and hybrid decisions

Owner: actual training authority; Engineering proves device/execution claims when they become relevant. Required decision: versioned signals/protocols/anchors, intensity/volume/effort, adequacy/materiality/necessity, benefit versus Change Cost, hysteresis/stickiness, spacing/tradeoffs, progression/deload/taper and valid substitutions, with accepted/rejected cases. No runtime proof is claimed here.

Review S01/S02 identical doses with flipped priority; ask whether KEEP is inadequate and why a smaller adjustment fails before MOVE. The example has12h end-to-start and13h start-to-start; specify the timing construct before comparing. Compare alternate row support/configuration without assuming lower cost or equal stimulus. Contrast squat3×5 versus5×8 only after effort/load/context are explicit. For running compare3×6min work with2min versus different recovery; distinguish intensity target from work completion. Reject universal spacing cutoffs, 'strides are metabolically high', fixed exercise-cost bands and muscle-only substitution. S13/S14 hold dose, schedule and priority constant and change only criticality.

Review S05 convergence and contrary signals; S12 extra set with directionally correct RIR; S13/S14 criticality; C2 order and next stimulus; C3 jointly valid balanced alternatives; C6 actual envelope and earned evidence. Supply C4 positive/negative level5/6 witnesses only if needed by the supported scope. C5 still needs a coordinated event-specific taper recipe; the phase label is not a dose. Acute performance effects and chronic adaptation require distinct endpoints/horizons. UNKNOWN cost bands may remain unused; do not invent a universal scale to fill the form.

## G03 — adherence and comparability

Owner: Product/UX with training professional. Required decision: modality/session-specific target revision, partial/tolerances, acceptable deviation, correction and no-sync cases. Raw factual recording does not need an automatic adherence judgment.

Inspect extra squat set; left/right doses;20s side-plank holds; partial interval/skip/recovery; accepted envelope versus outside; deliberately withdrawn dose versus unattempted target. Canonical fact revision2 supersedes revision1; manual+wearable delivery is one contribution. Unobserved past is unknown, not zero; valid future remainder is still projection. Completed prefix stays bound to executing revision after S29. Ask for accepted and rejected comparability examples (machine swap, different grade, effort absent) and separate adherence from performance/progression opportunity.

## G04 — restrictions and return

Owner: competent training professionals for nonclinical return, medical professional where clinical evaluation is needed; Engineering for operational failure. Required decision: concrete restriction/urgency/copy, eligibility/release/return classes and rules, versioned cases, re-evaluation and responsible person. No automatic clinical clearance by time/pain absence/HRV/user click.

S28 illustrates schedule interruption only, with restriction/eligibility unknown. Review nonclinical rebase and current context rather than restoring old load/pace or lost volume. Negative boundary: injury/illness, acute warning sign or uncertain eligibility requires the appropriate scoped process; do not invent diagnosis, clinical return dose, or a conservative workout as clearance. Actual clinical classes/copy need competent input; no attempt is made to preapprove them here.
