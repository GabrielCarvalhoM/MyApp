## 3. Test design — ASSUMED stimuli, no experiment executed

### Bands and hypotheses

All numbers below are **ASSUMED candidate stimuli**, chosen to span materially different commercial propositions, not estimated willingness to pay, a benchmark-derived recommendation or an official price. Founder retains the actual offer, eligibility, price and resource decision. M1/M2/M3 and Full/Crew Member are participation modes/roles, not commercial tiers. This design specifies no paywall, copy, layout or entitlement boundary. Safety, rights, disconnect and access to personal records cannot depend on coercive commercial choices; payment cannot be a prerequisite for another person's appropriate training or disclosure.

| Candidate market / currency | Monthly stimuli; range | Annual stimuli; range | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brazil / BRL | R$29.90 / R$44.90 / R$59.90; R$29.90–59.90 | R$239.90 / R$359.90 / R$479.90; R$239.90–479.90 | Independent local hypotheses; not conversion from USD or confirmation of an account-specific competing offer |
| US reference / USD | US$9.99 / US$14.99 / US$19.99; US$9.99–19.99 | US$79.99 / US$119.99 / US$159.99; US$79.99–159.99 | One international reference market, not a price for all countries; no assumed FX, tax or purchasing-power parity |

Annual candidates imply roughly one-third less than twelve corresponding monthly payments; that discount is also **ASSUMED**, not an established optimum. Begin with two monthly stimuli in one market; use the middle candidate only if the first contrast leaves a useful question. Annual billing is a later, separate choice: annual collection cannot establish monthly preference or renewal before an actual annual renewal falls due. Recheck regional/channel terms immediately before testing; never pool BRL and USD outcomes as one demand curve.

| Hypothesis / commercial option | Possible benefit | Trade-off and disconfirming evidence |
|---|---|---|
| H-C1: integrated coordination sustains a premium relative to logging alone | Higher contribution can fund credible service/support | Fewer buyers; novelty, Founder affinity and stated enthusiasm can mimic value. Compare net contribution and actual renewal, not comparator prices or preference alone. |
| H-C2: freemium participation lowers entry friction | More opportunities to experience value and invite known people | Free service has costs and may substitute for payment. No assumption that Engine is free/paid or every Crew participant becomes a buyer; incremental paid contribution must cover free usage. |
| H-C3: a **14-day, no-card trial (ASSUMED)** improves paid adoption | Allows experience before a deliberate purchase | Trial usage/provider/support costs accrue without revenue; more starts may yield fewer net payers. Compare with direct monthly purchase only after fixing price and delivered scope; no automatic charge is assumed. |
| H-C4: free Crew participation supports paying athletes; charging Crew participation funds its costs | Either can change acquisition, cost and continuity | Charging each member can deter invitations or create peer pressure; free members may never convert. Evaluate both as propositions before any access change. Neither authorizes extra Crew features or removal of canonical creation/participation rights. |

### Smallest decision-changing sequence

1. **Concept check, stated preference only.** Recruit opt-in adults from one accessible community who want coordinated Running × Strength, including solo people and people interested in a private Crew; record country/channel and recruitment source. A community is a recruitment source, not a new product community feature. Give each consenting participant one randomly allocated, truthful description of the same scoped offer at one of two monthly stimuli, with an explicit “would not buy” response. Keep capability, trial condition and researcher contact constant. Allocate/deliver at D0 and close responses at D7. Denominator: all eligible allocated participants; report response and stated acceptance separately, with nonresponse visible. Use this only to detect misunderstanding, objections and candidates for a real offer; no fake checkout, payment, WTP claim or product-efficacy claim.
2. **Real monthly offer, only when deliverable and separately authorized.** For a fresh cohort or a separately reported follow-up cohort, allocate two cost-feasible prices with the same service, terms and trial condition. Keep allocation fixed; no person sees a sequence of experimental prices. Recruitment/uptake closes at D7 after D0 allocation; observe first settled purchase through D28 and the first contractual monthly renewal through its due date + seven days, with an administrative cap at D70. A 14-day trial, if the approved offer uses it, begins by D7; trial start is never a paid conversion. No annual arm or trial/no-trial factorial here. A later trial comparison uses one fixed price, the same D70 horizon and the same renewal definition. Current documentary authorization executes none of these actions and does not permit delivery of unvalidated training/Nutrition guidance.
3. **Crew question only if it can change the free/paid decision.** First describe the two cost-bearing propositions from H-C4 without changing membership rights. If a causal Crew-adoption question remains after a viable monthly offer exists, randomize an optional invitation to use existing Crew Lite versus ordinary availability, at fixed price/service. Both arms retain solo value and existing rights; joining, inviting and sharing remain voluntary. This estimates the effect of encouragement in this cohort, not the effect of compulsory Crew membership or of a paid Crew plan. Do not compare joiners with nonjoiners and call the difference causal.

**Allocation and interference:** assign solo participants individually only when they have no enrolled Crew connection. Otherwise assign the entire connected component of enrolled, consenting multi-Crew memberships to one condition; never split overlapping Crews across price or encouragement arms. Use minimal enrolled IDs/membership information already authorized for this purpose; no contact ingestion, external social graph or observation of nonparticipants. Analyze by assigned component, with uncertainty based on independent components, not by treating correlated members as extra independent observations. Hidden links, shared invitations and conversations can still contaminate arms. If too few independent components are recruitable, keep a descriptive feasibility pilot and do not claim a causal Crew/price effect. Cross-country comparisons and sequential nonrandom cohorts remain observational. Payment, nonresponse and nonparticipation are never reported to peers; do not publish identifiable small-Crew results.

D7/D28/D70 are **ASSUMED observation windows** for this pilot, not validated training, retention or market thresholds. D0 is allocation, not a retrospectively selected activation date.

### Outcomes, denominators and controls

| Measure | Operational definition / window |
|---|---|
| Primary commercial outcome | Contribution earned over D0–D70 per eligible randomized participant (or summed component contribution divided by its allocated participants), including costs of free users, trialists and nonbuyers; compare assigned arms within market. Use §2's recognized-revenue/cost definition and show fixed-cost scenarios separately. A higher paid conversion rate alone does not win. |
| Purchase / renewal | People with a settled, not fully refunded/reversed first payment, and people with a settled, not fully refunded/reversed first renewal, each divided by **all eligible randomized participants**. Partial refunds reduce contribution. Also report renewal divided by buyers whose first renewal became due; mark this conditional denominator and right-censor not-yet-due buyers. Refund exposure after cutoff remains a limitation. |
| Exposure / uptake | Log offer version, assigned arm/unit, country/channel, allocation and actual delivery time; missing delivery is a failure to reach, retained in the primary intention-to-treat denominator and shown separately. Trial starts, Crew invitation acceptance and stated preference are separate intermediate outcomes, never payment or delivered value. |
| Value / retention guardrail | At D28 and D70 ask the person about their own useful coordination and appropriate continuity, including adaptation, rest and return. Missing response stays missing. Do not infer correctness from app opens, workout volume, an emitted event, another member's report or an absence list; self-report is a supporting, nonclinical signal. |
| Costs / harm | Track net receipts/refunds, provider/compute/support usage and fixed commitments by arm; include trial/free cost even with zero revenue and avoid included-credit double-counting. Record volunteered pressure/unwanted disclosure, billing confusion and cancellation/access failures through private support. No health/Nutrition/recovery data or peer-compliance scoring is needed for this commercial test. |

Exclude before allocation only ineligible age/market, no valid consent, duplicates, staff/test accounts and conflicts with the deliverable service's approved population; record reasons/counts. Deduplicate by enrolled person, preserve assignment on repeat contacts and use timestamps/timezone consistently. After allocation, nonpurchase, cancellation, disconnection, nonadherence, refunds and dissatisfaction are outcomes, not exclusions. Handle consent withdrawal/deletion as required; report resulting missingness without silently replacing people. Self-selection, community/Founder affinity, prior survey anchoring, novelty, seasonality, language/channel, cluster imbalance and unobserved cross-Crew contact limit transportability and inference.

**Before launch, not a new analytics project:** Data and Engineering reconcile a test exposure, a payment, a refund, a renewal and a duplicate against the actual delivery/billing records; verify offer/version, identity, time, deduplication and arm linkage. Security verifies the applicable consent/access/retention arrangement. No collection exists or has been validated now. A schema, full taxonomy or dashboard is unnecessary for this decision. Data freezes the roster, allocation, attainable precision and analysis cutoff before seeing outcomes; required independent units depend on the economically meaningful contrast and available recruitment, not a universal sample size or significance rule.

**Decision and stop:** run one precommitted cohort to its cutoff, with no optional early winner-picking or repeated price fishing. Report counts, costs, arm differences and uncertainty; if plausible results cover both economic loss and meaningful gain, the result is inconclusive. Advance only an offer with defensible contribution after trial/free costs and fixed-cost scenarios and no unresolved safety/privacy/billing harm; positive pilot contribution still does not establish acquisition economics or long-term retention. Pause the affected arm immediately for an unauthorized disclosure/charge, coercive access change, inability to cancel or a credible service-safety issue; its owner resolves the cause before resumption. Stop acquisition at the approved budget/recruitment cap; do not delete observed failures. No positive result changes scope, safety policy or commercial authority by itself.

**Exact remaining conditions:** Founder chooses actual price/offer/trial/free boundary and resource cap before a live commitment; Engineering/operations supply deliverable service, production billing/provider entitlement and measured costs; Data verifies collection and fixes precision from that actual roster; Security clears the applicable collection boundary. An unavailable wearable cost/entitlement or unvalidated professional policy blocks only the dependent live service/test claim. None of these open commercial conditions blocks UX/Figma, and this author contribution is not its own independent measurement gate.

## 4. Disposition

Research stop: enough primary offers to form test hypotheses; additional competitor tables would not establish Vilumo willingness to pay. Exact final offer, monetization/free boundary, trial conditions and target contribution require Founder decision and observed experiments. Prices/fees must be rechecked at experiment/release time. Commercial uncertainty blocks committing a billing implementation and launch economics, **not UX/Figma**. No new pricing authority or product scope is created by this pack.
