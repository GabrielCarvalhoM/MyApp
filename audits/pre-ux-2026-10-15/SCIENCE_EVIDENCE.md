**Decision supported:** specify qualitative coordination classes, contextual information, uncertainty and abstention sufficiently for structural UX. **Decision not supported:** autonomously prescribe training, return from illness/injury, individual diet/fluid doses or universal thresholds. Literature supports a decision framework; it does not validate Vilumo's future algorithm, efficacy, injury prevention or target population.

Method: focused search by decision, using original journal publications, original consensus/position statements and author institutional records. Search families: concurrent training/order/acute effects; load and ACWR including contrary evidence; RPE/RIR/well-being/sleep/HRV; deload/detraining; pre/during/post/event nutrition and hydration; REDs. Recent 2025–2026 publications were checked where they could change the conclusion. This is a bounded evidence synthesis, **not** a registered/exhaustive systematic review. Sources indexed more than once and overlapping meta-analyses are not independent replications. No blogs, competitor marketing, Reddit or AI output support the claims.

**STOP_WHEN:** all A/B requested topics have an applicable evidence statement or an explicit evidence/authority gap; qualitative actions and non-automation are actionable for documentary consumers; additional accessible papers would not change those choices. This threshold is reached for this documentary scope. Searching indefinitely cannot supply a named licensed professional, validate a product policy or prove provider measurements. Resume research only for a specified policy/population/content decision that could change.

Epistemic labels: **OBSERVED** = reported finding within the publication's population/design, not product telemetry; **INFERRED** = explicit translation with limited external validity; **DECIDED** = Founder/baseline rule; **PROPOSED** = operational recommendation for owner review; **ASSUMED** = unvalidated premise. Confidence is qualitative and claim-specific; it is not a probability or runtime readiness score.

## 4. Qualitative action policy interface (proposal, not approved physiology)

These are **classes an eventual policy can execute once its inputs, applicability, rules and alternatives are approved**. Research does not choose the dose, cutoff, permissible exercise substitution or medical triage. Input states sufficient/partial/conflicting/stale/absent describe evidence **for a specific decision**, not the athlete's health. A complete watch record can still be insufficient to prescribe. An incomplete optional metric can still permit an otherwise supported decision.

| Class | Candidate situation and minimum reasoning | Admissible documentary outcome / required limit |
|---|---|---|
| **MAINTAIN** | Current plan remains applicable, required context is adequate and no material conflict/hold is unresolved. TR-001, 007–012. | Keep the validated planned session; explain material uncertainty. Do not escalate load merely because a score is high. “No new data” alone neither triggers change nor certifies safety. |
| **MOVE / REORDER** | A scheduling constraint or supported competing session demand threatens declared priority; alternative placement fits available time and both modalities. TR-002–005. | Propose explicit affected sessions/windows and priority trade-off; confirmation per AEC. No universal spacing requirement; if no feasible alternative, ask context/preference or a qualified policy chooses another admissible action. |
| **REDUCE_VOLUME** | The governing policy supports reducing the amount of work while retaining the relevant session purpose. TR-003–006, 014. | Show which work changes and effects on remaining plan. No percentage, duration, sets or progression invented by Research/LLM. Retaining high intensity is not an automatic safe default. |
| **REDUCE_INTENSITY** | The governing policy supports lowering demand/effort while retaining an appropriate amount/type of work. TR-003–006, 014. | Distinct from volume; specify intensity construct (pace/target/relative load/effort), as approved. No “slower” or “lighter” command without validated bounds and context. |
| **SUBSTITUTE** | Original session purpose can be served by an alternative within approved repertoire, capability/equipment and safety coverage. TR-002–006. | Explain retained/lost purpose and cross-modal consequences. No automatic different sport, novel eccentric exercise, pain workaround or supposedly equal calories/distance. If no approved alternative, abstain. |
| **RECOVERY / REST** | Supported reduced-stress/recovery strategy or confirmed rest selected under qualified policy; person's choice remains valid. TR-010–015. | Do not equate active recovery, deload, full rest and clinical hold. Evidence here does not license an automatic active-recovery session when risks/context are unknown. No social penalty or missed-session debt. |
| **REQUEST_CONTEXT** | A missing/conflicting/materially stale fact can change action, e.g. actual unplanned load, goal priority, local functional limitation or relevant restriction. TR-005–013. | Ask the smallest decision-changing item; allow “unknown/prefer not to answer.” Avoid mandatory daily batteries or dietary diaries. State which recommendation is limited; optional absent wearable alone is not a hold. |
| **NO_AUTO_ADAPT / HOLD** | Required safety/applicability fact unresolved, critical data corrupted/conflicting, restriction/red flag, unsupported population/decision or repeated policy failure. TR-005–015/NUT-009. | Suspend the affected unsupported recommendation and new effects/exports, preserve history and agency, explain scope and next action. A high score/user confirmation cannot clear a professional hold. Logging/correction and unrelated permissible functions need not disappear. Trigger/exit/escalation must be owned; no generic “do something conservative” prescription. |
| **RECONCILE / REPLAN / RETURN** | Missed/partial/unplanned/corrected activity or interruption changes the current context, after identifying the actual fact. TR-013/015. | Recalculate the future under current approved policy, preserving given/executed versions. A logistical move is not proof of physiological equivalence; an interruption due to illness/injury cannot be resolved by ordinary missed-session logic. |

**Minimum context contract to enable future policy:** user-declared goals and priority; experience and recent practices; session modality/purpose/demand/targeted region/novelty when known; planned and actual time/exposure; available windows/equipment; observation provenance and coverage; relevant self-reported exertion/local function/general recovery; material restrictions and policy eligibility. Exact questions/scales/frequency, red-flag list and retention are not fixed here. Request each only if needed for that decision, with purpose/consent. No inference of complete health from silence.

**Priority semantics:** first-class Running/Strength does not imply equal dose, equal priority each day or maximizing both simultaneously. Protect the declared primary outcome within safety/applicability, keep the secondary supported within available resources, and surface the trade-off. If priorities or acceptable sacrifice are undeclared and decisive, ask. This is an operational inference from TR-001/002, not a Founder strategy change.

### 5.1 Three output classes, sufficiency and non-automation

These are proposed content/decision boundaries for AEC §6/RS/CF F02/F06; the authoritative owners adopt exact wording after professional-boundary review. **No personalized diet prescription is authorized by this mission.** A scientific range in a paper would be an envelope for its stated population, never a Vilumo MUST. Numeric doses are deliberately omitted because they do not resolve this mission's decisions and would require a validated personal policy/authority.

| Output class | Context sufficient for this purpose | Permitted documentary behavior / abstention |
|---|---|---|
| **EDUCATION** | Curated source-backed topic with visible applicability/limits. Session link may explain why topic is relevant; unknown intake remains unknown. | General explanation of fueling/recovery/hydration, without individual need/deficit/dose/food plan. User can dismiss. Omit if irrelevant, potentially misleading or under a restriction; “general education” is not a legal safe harbour. |
| **CONTEXTUAL PERFORMANCE GUIDANCE** | Current session/version, nature/expected duration and relative demand, relevant phase (pre/during/post/short turnaround/event), next demand/time if material, basic opportunity/tolerance/restriction context needed by an approved content rule, and valid purpose/consent. | Select/reframe an approved non-prescriptive message for that specific demand, with provenance, limits and actionability. This context is sufficient **to select content**, not to determine grams, portions, fluid replacement, deficiency or a clinical clearance. Selection policy and exact content need qualified/professional review before real users. |
| **INDIVIDUALIZED PRESCRIPTION** | Would require qualified individual assessment, appropriate authority/legal model and a separately approved policy; extra fields alone do not satisfy it. | Personalized quantities/timing/portions, individualized carbohydrate/protein/fluid/electrolyte targets, diet/energy restriction, clinical nutrition or supplements do not become autonomous V1 behavior. Hold/defer to the appropriate professional, preserve an educational path only when suitable. No new in-app consultation or professional-plan feature is created here. |

**Purpose-limited context:** session demand/phase; current versus upcoming plan; interval to next demanding session; voluntary high-level statement about existing preparation, tolerance, access or relevant restriction when needed. Do not demand food diaries, weigh-ins, calories, macro totals, full medical histories or menstrual tracking to display a general insight. Do not infer disease, pregnancy, medication use or disordered eating from training patterns; an explicit restriction/out-of-domain disclosure routes to scoped hold rather than collecting everything. Professional policy decides exact eligibility and safe wording.

**Surface timing:** choose relevance from the current demand, not a universal time window. Pre: enough lead time for the permitted action. During: only applicable content/demand with prior tolerability/opportunity context. Post: actual session and upcoming demands, not merely a completed checkbox. Short turnaround: next session/time known and approved rule applicable. Event: advance preparation/practice, never last-minute novel protocol. Update or dismiss obsolete insight after any material plan/session/context revision; it is not an eaten/consumed fact or evidence of adherence.

**Abstain or restrict:** unknown critical demand/phase; conflicting stale session; unknown context that changes advice; medical/dietary restriction or unsupported condition; inability to identify a qualified/content-authorized option. Ask only the material question or offer suitable general education/omit. Do not fill an obligatory card with “safe” dose/food/timing invented by an LLM. A disclaimer or checkbox cannot clear the missing authority. No Nutrition/Recovery/reason enters Crew/share.
