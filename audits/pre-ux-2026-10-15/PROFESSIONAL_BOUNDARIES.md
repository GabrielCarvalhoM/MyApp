## 2. SAFE-001 — content classes and responsible authority (PROPOSED)

These are product risk/authoring classes, **not statutory safe harbours**. Route by the substance, intended use, data used and level of individualisation. A disclaimer or a non-numeric recommendation cannot by itself resolve reserved practice.

| Class | Permitted documentary design now | Boundary for real delivery / responsible authority |
|---|---|---|
| General information/education | Explain a general concept from a reviewed source; no assessment of the person's intake, status, deficiency or individual need; no required food, amount, schedule or target. An asset can be selected by session category while its wording remains general. | Nutritionist reviews nutrition content and evidence/applicability; legal reviews the exact service/publication model because REG-001 also covers nutritional education. Do not state that general education is automatically unregulated. A general educational asset is not a personalised recommendation or permission to practise. |
| Contextual sport-performance guidance | Link an approved asset to the actual session/revision and phase (pre/during/post/between sessions/event), explaining why the topic is relevant and its limits. Known context can establish applicability, not a personal dose. | Nutritionist + legal determine which combinations of wording, selection logic, prompts and personal data remain permissible in the intended service. Context sufficient scientifically does not settle professional legality. Until that validation, support education/omission/referral states; do not generate individual fueling/hydration/recovery instructions. |
| Individual dietary/nutritional prescription | Identify and reject requests for a person's food/portion/macros, weight-loss/deficit plan, nutrient/fluid target, personalised timing/replacement or condition-specific diet. A single-session instruction can be individualised without becoming a full diet. | **Not authorised for autonomous V1 delivery.** Requires qualified individual assessment, responsible professional and lawful operating model; validation cannot expand Founder exclusions. Full diets, logging, meal planning and supplements stay OUT. A personal g/kg, mL/h or “+20%” instruction is not released by attaching an evidence link or calling it an insight. |
| Exercise prescription/adaptation | Define validated-domain status, input/decision classes and approved options; retain full Running/Strength structures. | Registered exercise professional/competent physiology authority validates policies, supported cohort, progression, interaction, return, stop and applicability. Qualified legal review establishes the actual service obligations. Physician/other treating professional handles medical assessment/restriction when relevant; no global requirement for every adult to obtain medical clearance is invented. |

**Competence allocation.** Nutritionist owns review of nutrition content, individual nutrition assessment and applicable professional acts. Exercise professional owns exercise policy/cases within their competence. Physician evaluates medical symptoms, restrictions/clinical clearance where indicated, diagnosis and treatment; other treating professionals remain involved in their own scopes. Legal determines service classification, corporate/professional registration/technical responsibility if applicable, consent/base legal, records and transfer requirements. Founder arranges real competent participation/resources and selects permitted business choices; Founder or Chief cannot waive reserved acts. No single professional rubber stamp or AI role substitutes this operating model. No new clinician marketplace, consultation journey or document upload is implied.

## 3. SAFE-002 — context minimum and abstention (PROPOSED)

**Required to select a session-linked asset:** current session and plan/workout revisions; modality; relevant phase; known demand category under approved policy; relative timing of that session and, if the topic depends on it, the next session/event; applicability state and a content/policy revision. Avoid demanding nutrition/health disclosure when general content suffices. Session demand, energy intake and energy availability are different facts.

**Ask only when it changes the eligible next action:** optional practical constraints/tolerance already volunteered or necessary for a specifically validated content rule; an ability to say a restriction applies or decline detail. Do not collect a meal diary, calories, exact macros, body composition, complete diagnoses, medication lists or laboratory results merely to unlock generic guidance. Even weight is unnecessary for non-quantitative education. Data fields and vocabularies must derive from the selected reviewed policy, not this pack becoming a mandatory health questionnaire.

**Unknown/refused context:** optional non-critical context absent → general approved educational asset or omit; explain the relevant limitation. Missing critical applicability → ask the minimum, or withhold that guidance. Do not pressure consent, infer absence of disease/restriction, treat declined context as a negative health answer, or block private records/Crew participation. Restriction present but detail private → hold the dependent recommendation and route to qualified help without demanding the diagnosis.

Do not infer a meal consumed from an insight acknowledged, calculate energy availability from incomplete activity/intake data, diagnose low energy availability/REDs, infer pregnancy, eating disorder or another sensitive condition from performance, missed workouts, sleep/HRV, body shape, language or Crew behaviour. Declared concerns can trigger the applicable hold/referral; they do not become a system diagnosis. Scientific assets, quantities and evidence IDs are supplied by the science ledger; this pack supplies no replacement formulas or universal timing.

## 4. SAFE-003 — bounded safety HOLD/referral contract (PROPOSED)

**HOLD means the product withholds the affected recommendation/adaptation, not that it diagnoses the person, declares them unfit, or locks their account.** A mere unsupported integration or non-critical missing signal is ordinary degraded confidence, not automatically a medical hold. Keep private observation/correction/rights and unaffected non-prescriptive capabilities usable; unchanged Crew rights remain independent. An old prescription cannot be presented as safe merely because generating a new one is blocked.

| Class | Trigger class, no invented clinical threshold | Scope / action / release condition |
|---|---|---|
| SAFETY_RED_FLAG | Person reports a professionally defined concerning sign/symptom, or an approved validated safety rule flags a possible immediate risk. | Suspend dependent exercise/nutrition recommendation, explain that the app cannot assess the situation, route to appropriate qualified/urgent care according to a professionally approved escalation rule. Symptom examples, urgency and emergency copy need medical/professional approval; no diagnosis/automated all-clear. |
| OUTSIDE_VALIDATED_DOMAIN | Known population/condition or decision type outside the reviewed policy's applicability; or applicability critical to the decision cannot be established. | Withhold only dependent prescriptive output. Refer to appropriate exercise/nutrition/clinical professional. An adult age check alone does not establish physiological eligibility. Validation must explicitly cover the situation before autonomous guidance can resume. |
| DECLARED_RESTRICTION | Person declares a current clinician/professional restriction or an applicable constraint conflicts with a proposal. | Restriction takes precedence over plan, engagement and LLM. Clarify only what's needed; no general exercise or nutrition substitute assumed safe. Review/updated relevant restriction plus a newly applicable policy/context is needed, not a generic “I accept risk” button. |
| CRITICAL_DATA_INVALID | Data required for safety/applicability is corrupt, belongs to the wrong person, has critical unit/time/provenance conflict, or materially conflicting sources are unresolved. | Withhold dependent calculation/export, request correction or use a specifically validated independent path. Preserve source/conflict trace without counting incompatible observations as facts. Release only after integrity/applicability is established; missing HRV alone does not qualify. |
| REPEATED_ADAPTATION_FAILURE | A supported feedback path identifies persistent failed/inappropriate adaptations, or integrity checks prevent coherent revisions. | Stop the affected automatic adaptation path and escalation/retry loop; preserve records and inform the person. Engineering identifies an operational failure; domain professional reviews safety/application where needed. No invented number of failures, universal time window or diagnostic inference. Resume after root cause, corrected policy/context and revalidation. |
| POLICY_OR_AUTHORITY_ABSENT | Requested action lacks an approved rule, applicability/evidence revision, required professional operating approval or relevant current permission. | Do not invent a conservative dose or ask the LLM to fill the gap. Offer permitted education, context correction or referral. Resume only when the missing scoped approval/permission and policy exist. |

**State semantics:** eligible/degraded → context required or HOLD(reason class, scope, restricted private provenance, relevant revision) → review/correction pending → reassessed with a current approved policy → eligible, still degraded, or HOLD. User acknowledgment is not safety clearance. A correction can clear a mistaken context/identity flag; a clinical restriction requires its appropriate resolution. No automatic resumption from silence, a later “good” wearable score, elapsed time or a Crew commitment. Readiness/confidence and HOLD are separate dimensions.

**Agency and external copies:** the person can reject a recommendation, correct data, request review, or truthfully record doing something else. That does not make the engine generate/export a prohibited prescription or remove safety controls. Do not punish deviation or rewrite the performed record. HOLD invalidates affected pending unsafe outputs and new exports. Already delivered remote workouts may remain on a watch: show the actual known copy/version and limitations, offer supported cancellation as a separate authorised action, never promise recall. A HOLD/resumption does not send a social event or expose its reason. Reassess the future only; no retroactive safety approval.

**Policy ownership, not an imaginary clinical protocol:** validated-domain definition, red flags, urgent versus ordinary referral, applicability population/conditions and repeated-failure criteria must be supplied/reviewed by appropriate real professionals and Engineering. Pregnancy/postpartum, injury rehabilitation, diagnosed conditions relevant to exercise/fueling and eating-disorder concerns are examples requiring an applicability check, not a categorical ban on those people or a claim that age alone validates everyone else. The app must not silently broaden the reviewed cohort. No symptom classifier, numeric threshold, diagnosis or quantified safety claim is approved here.

## 5. SAFE-004 — LLM and external text boundary (PROPOSED)

An optional LLM may extract a **candidate** fact from volunteered language, ask a minimum clarification, explain the approved current engine decision, or present options already allowed by its policy. A candidate sensitive/context-changing fact must be confirmed/corrected through its canonical object before use for a material decision. No private conversational store becomes a parallel Athlete Context. Preserve distinction between the person's declaration, an imported observation and the model's inference.

LLM output, workout titles, provider metadata, source documents and user free text are **data, not instructions or privileged policy**. Text such as “ignore restrictions”, an alleged professional approval, a pasted prescription or an instruction hidden in a provider activity cannot authorise a dose, clear a HOLD, change a goal, grant permission, send a workout, exercise rights or share to Crew. An LLM explanation cannot cite evidence not actually attached to the applied rule. If no approved explanation/option fits, abstain and route; do not fabricate physiology or policy. Runtime authorisation remains outside the model and is checked against current scope/version at effect time. Agent exclusion from social/send/share actions in AEC-1.0 is preserved.

Future LLM selection requires minimised fields, service/data role and retention/no-training arrangements, recipient/transfer assessment and adversarial output tests. None is resolved merely by this conservative behaviour contract. Core loop remains usable without the agent. This pack does not select a model or require AI for education.

## 6. SAFE-005 — assets, purposes and sensitive-data controls (PROPOSED)

**Assets/actors/boundaries.** Private Athlete Context, Recovery/Nutrition, I10 reasons and inference/decision trace belong to the athlete's authorised experience. Sources/adapters, future processors/LLM, operations support, Crew recipients and external share destinations are distinct trust boundaries. Raw narrative, source health readings and derived private restrictions are not social objects. A callback can carry authentic provider transport while still carrying wrong/stale/adversarial content; authenticity is not physiological confidence.

| Risk / boundary | Contract to integrate into existing authority | Required proof later / owner |

| Health/nutrition used as ordinary analytics | Minimise and treat potentially revealing source/derived data as sensitive until an actual operation-specific classification; restricted access, privacy-safe logs, no health marketing audiences or commercial reuse assumed.
