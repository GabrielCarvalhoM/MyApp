## What each documents

- **RN1 Not Feeling 100%.** For feeling "not your best" (minor illness, stress, fatigue, work, mild aches). 3–14 days, today or scheduled, back-dated up to 7 days, editable, a reminder before it ends. Four intensity levels (reduced difficulty; easy and long runs only; short easy runs only; no running), and strength can be removed temporarily. Three return speeds (slowly: minor aches; balanced: a cold or short illness; quickly: busy periods or time off), computed by Runna and changeable. Based on how the user feels, not a diagnosis. For more serious injury or illness, or more than 14 days away: a proper break, a healthcare professional, and a new plan when ready. "Coaching advice and not a substitute for medical advice." History preservation is not documented.
- **RN2 Recovery.** Not Feeling 100% pauses progression for up to 14 days; the return has up to three stages (short easy runs only, then easy and long runs, then full training with speed), stage length depending on how long the user felt unwell. After a complete holiday break, first-week mileage is capped; after two or more weeks without a long run, the long run is capped. Missed sessions: plans "adapt" (no detail). Pain or discomfort: seek medical advice.
- **TR1 Training Adjustments.** Types Time Off, Injury, Illness; a date range and optional description; overlapping workouts prompt a plan adaptation, which aims to avoid "something too hard right after your time off". Changing the date range re-adapts; changing type or text does not. Deleting an adjustment re-adapts, accounting for recent training. More than 14 consecutive days without a completed workout: the user "may want to re-test" (a ramp test may be scheduled). Injury: consult a medical professional; illness: rest and, if possible, see a medical professional. Severity, missed-workout accumulation and history preservation are not documented on the page read.
- **GS1 Statuses.** Active (default), On a Break, Sick, Injured. Sick, Injured and On a Break pause push notifications for workout suggestions and activity reminders; back to Active restarts them. The app treats the break as intentional, not as lost motivation; guidance stays "more relevant". Effects on streaks or scores, penalties, history and medical advice are not documented on that page.
- **GA1 Training status.** Pause when injured or sick; activities can still be recorded; training status, training load focus, recovery feedback and workout recommendations are temporarily disabled. Resume manually "when you are ready to start training again". Plan adaptation after time away is not documented in the source read.
- **IOC (abstract).** A clinician-facing consensus for SEM clinicians on acute respiratory infections; return-to-sport guidelines are among its topics. Read only as scope: its population is athletes with respiratory infection, managed by clinicians.
- **BERN (abstract).** Return to sport is a continuum alongside recovery and rehabilitation, decided ideally collaboratively by clinicians, athletes and coaches; evidence for return decisions is scarce. (The continuum's three elements — return to participation, to sport, to performance — come from its full text, not read here; the Founder's request states the same distinction.)

## What the benchmark shows for F08

- Every product documented here **leaves the clinical decision to the user and a professional**, with a disclaimer. None documents verifying a clearance or encoding a professional's restrictions. Where illness or injury is serious, the documented pattern is to stop the plan and start a new one later (RN1), or to pause and resume manually (GA1).
- The engine cannot copy the self-managed parts (patterns 4, 5, 12) for clinical causes: its canon makes clearance and clinical return the competence of qualified professionals (PDR G04; DR-99; SSCTR constitution, SSCTR-02/06), and a statement that passes a format check is not a medical authorization.
- The patterns it already implements (1, 2, 6, 8, 9, 11, 13, 14) need no change. The F08 gap is the one transition none of the competitors documents either: a **legitimate, scoped clearance** that opens a sporting reassessment without restoring the old plan.
