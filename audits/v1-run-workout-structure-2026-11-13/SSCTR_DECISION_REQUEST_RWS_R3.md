# SSCTR decision request r3 — distance as the completion criterion (RWS-C), 2026-11-13

Requester: Chief. Bounded review of a **Founder product decision**, not a request to reopen it.

**Founder decision (binding product rule):** when the CoachingCore prescribes a native distance target, the distance is the completion criterion. 16 km long run: met after 16 km. 9 km easy run: met after 9 km. 6 × 800 m: each repetition targets 800 m. Recovery by distance advances by its distance. **No automatic time ceiling as an alternative completion condition.** Duration may be estimated and shown, never a completion criterion. Before prescribing, the distance must be appropriate to capacity, history, load, goal and availability; if not, a shorter distance or an explicit time session. Novices and returning athletes may get time or justified mixed sessions. The athlete may stop at any time (fatigue, discomfort, own decision); the real execution is recorded and the target is not declared met. Completion statuses: DISTANCE, TIME, PARTIAL, STOPPED, UNKNOWN. No absence of measurement as success; no demand for absolute GPS precision.
