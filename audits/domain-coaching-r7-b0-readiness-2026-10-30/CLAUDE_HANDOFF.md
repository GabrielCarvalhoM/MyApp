## Already-decided V1 constraints

- **Running + Strength**, both first class, one athlete/model and one integrated final plan (C023/C045/C046). Crew/internal social is **DEFERRED, NOT REJECTED**; voluntary external sharing does not make an internal social network. Cycling/swimming and new sport programming are outside V1.
- **iOS only; Apple Health/HealthKit is the required external health/activity input** (DR-120–123). Manual and in-app records remain valid. Core consumes canonical facts with provenance and capability flags; missing sets, reps, load, RIR or run detail stay unknown. Direct vendors, Health Connect/Android and structured watch delivery are POST_V1. HealthKit write-back is open at iOS prep, not a B0 blocker.
- **Priority is dynamic/contextual**, derived from goals, phase/event, evidence and a temporary preference where material. No required static Run-first/Strength-first/Balanced user flag, secret runner-first, 50/50 split or alternating winner. R6 Founder request §4 explicitly rejects the old static assumption; R6 policy and hybrid/balanced documents operationalize it. Genuine incomparable trade-offs ASK.
- **Pace is an admissible prescription representation** (RIL §§2–3 and this direct request). A usable target requires an approved method tied to evidence/current capacity, construct, date/version, uncertainty and comparability. An aspirational race-time goal or easy pace does not establish threshold/training pace. R6 uses effort/calibration cues and lacks pace prediction. [R2-P14](../../CoachingCore/r2/domain/ACF_G05_RUN_CALIBRATION.md) separately documents bounded reuse of a familiar demonstrated field-session target; check its positive registry + independent authority, not the candidate label. Reuse is not new-family calibration, physiological equivalence or broader R6 coverage.
- **Chosen days/turns/time per sport and long-run day are strong constraints** (DR-112). Set weekly load first and distribute it: extra days add no load. Do not silently move/drop chosen days or fill them with invalid training. Explain a covered lower-demand option/retention and negotiate infeasibility. Shared turns mean explicitly confirmed back-to-back sessions; key-session order follows context, ASK when both are key and preference is decisive.
- **Stability is intentional** (L024/C057–60): learn continuously; preserve a valid adequate plan; progress from comparable earned evidence; use purposeful programmed variation; react within a cycle only to material inadequacy with the minimum effective change. Novelty and elapsed time earn nothing.
- **Rotation is a preference**, not evidence-optimal cadence (DR-113). Coach decides / 6 weeks / 2 weeks / fixed concern selection only; preserve essential stimulus and establish a new exercise's load by approved effort guidance, without transferring another exercise's load. Fixed permits eligible substitutions with agency. Taper/race-week rotation waits. R6 rotates accessories and retains main lifts; the broader two-week main-variant question is unresolved.
- **Metadata carries decisions**, not decorative labels (STIL §§2/5–7): stable exercise/configuration/revision, full equipment/increments, pattern and muscle roles, laterality/side, ROM/skill where relevant, load convention, preparation versus work sets, effort/rest/order, eligibility and substitution relations. Axial/eccentric/novelty/local/systemic demand depends on dose, athlete and evidence; no fixed fatigue truth, universal recovery timer or muscle-overlap equivalence. Count each exposure once; planned + observed and primary + secondary muscles do not multiply systemic load.
- **Representation:** PT-BR/EN/ES (DR-103), explicit canonical quantities plus display units/preferences (DR-114), pace/speed inverse range order, kg/lb and TOTAL/PER_HAND/machine/assistance/bodyweight distinctions. Locale is not a sporting rule. R6's pound rounding/display/increments remain a representation limitation to map, not a new dose policy.
- **Marathon is not supported by the current reviewed repertoire scope.** C049 lists it only as a conditional objective example; R6 policy exclusions and cold-start/event documents expressly exclude marathon/ultra and cover 10K/half-marathon events. Do not add marathon or turn a broad Run family name into approved V1 event coverage. Any expansion needs explicit scope plus applicable domain review; this pack supplies neither.

## Fixed coaching semantics to preserve

History, projection and the per-decision PlanningView are distinct. Only factual evidence writes history; a released step is not executed. Nonclinical **return/restart** starts from current evidence with prior tolerance as ceiling, never a universal rollback or automatic restoration. Interruption identity and its factual starting point persist across corrections; a genuinely new interruption gets a new identity, ambiguity ASK. E1 distinguishes short versus moderate/long recipes and caps ladder/restart/return at the step last executed; EASY uses the largest easy/long doses executed and tolerated since entry, not simply the last phase-reduced run. Use the exact contract, not this compressed sentence, for a later consumer.

**HOLD is overloaded:** AEC §7.1 suspends dependent advice/effects with cause, scope, owner and exit condition; it is not a diagnosis/account block or automatic approval of an old dependent prescription. The progression gate HOLD repeats the current valid dose within its range and earns no future progression. **MAINTAIN** is goal/context-specific, not frozen old projection: decision-authority §C preserves the example of factual 70×10, projected 70×12, new maintain target 8 → 70×8. Missing data is not poor tolerance; rest is not failure or volume debt. Clinical pain/injury/illness/rehabilitation/clearance stays human-required; unknown eligibility asks and retains only the dependent scope.
