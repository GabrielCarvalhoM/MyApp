# Privacy by design and LGPD preparation

Foundation v1 · 2026-10-30. This is a technical planning baseline, not legal advice or a claim of LGPD compliance. Product/Engineering own data-flow implementation; Privacy/Security review it. A qualified legal/DPO review must resolve lawful bases, controller/operator roles, notices, retention, processors/transfers and data-subject procedures **before real collection/processing that depends on them**. No real-user data is introduced here.

The [official LGPD text](https://presidencia.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709compilado.htm) includes health-related data among sensitive personal data, purpose/necessity principles and data-subject rights. Treat health and health-revealing training inputs/derived inferences as sensitive for engineering controls until reviewed; ordinary account identifiers remain personal data. Route/location and photos may be highly identifying even where their legal category depends on context. Pseudonymous identifiers and linked hashes remain personal data when reidentification/linkage is possible.

## Lifecycle and UX boundaries

Collect only fields needed for a named current purpose; optionality and missingness are modeled, not filled with inferred health facts. Default backend sync, analytics enrichment and secondary use to off/unimplemented until individually approved. Do not retain raw HealthKit payloads merely because a normalized fact is useful. Keep provenance and uncertainty only at the minimum granularity needed by the authorized domain behavior. Do not repurpose health data for advertising, unrelated profiling or model training through an SDK default.

Explain purpose and benefit before a contextual permission request; request the minimum HealthKit data types and distinguish platform authorization, app settings and legal consent. No bundled permission/consent wall or implication that an OS dialog is the lawful basis for every server use. [Apple's HealthKit documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/authorizing-access-to-health-data) states that read-grant/denial is not directly revealed to the app. Empty results mean unavailable/unknown, not proven denial; do not nag or fabricate an authorization state. Preserve usable authorized alternatives under the product contract.

Revocation is purpose-specific and easy to reach. Stop future processing/sync for revoked purposes, cancel queued work and revoke external/session access as applicable. Reassess already collected/derived data against its remaining purpose and basis; revocation alone does not resolve every retention/deletion question. Update ledger/choice versions and ensure reinstall, background retries and restore cannot revive a withdrawn choice silently.

HealthKit disconnect must stop app-side queries/background delivery and sync and invalidate app-held anchors/caches as appropriate; explain that users manage OS permissions in Health settings. App disconnect, deleting the app's imported copies and deleting original Apple Health samples are distinct operations. Do not delete source Health records without separately authorized supported behavior; current write-back remains undecided. Deletion/suppression markers must prevent reimport of intentionally removed app records until a valid new user instruction and policy permits it.

Account deletion requires verified principal and appropriate reauthentication, clear scope/consequences, idempotent job handling and status/failure recovery. Revoke sessions, stop imports/background jobs, remove account/health/derived records across stores/caches/processors and expire exports. Inventory exceptional retention with purpose/access/expiry and legal/DPO review; do not claim immediate physical erasure from immutable backups. Retention expiry plus tombstone replay after restore must prevent serving deleted data. Explain separately that deleting an account does not itself cancel an App Store subscription.

Export is principal-scoped, rate-limited, auditable and delivered encrypted over an authenticated channel or short-lived one-time link; never a public bucket or guessed account URL. Define machine-readable data/provenance/units/schema and coverage honestly, including approved derived records and corrections; exclude other users, credentials and privileged internal material. Decide legal scope and deadlines with legal/DPO rather than promising them here. Reauthenticate and test link expiry, wrong-account access, repeated jobs, cleanup and processor involvement.

Correction permits supported changes to inaccurate/incomplete user data with provenance, versioning and reconciliation. Do not rewrite observed history as though it never happened; distinguish correction/supersession from newly observed facts. Recompute only warranted derived state and propagate correction/deletion to approved recipients. Changes to historical/sporting authority need existing domain gates; privacy rights are not permission for infrastructure code to alter the frozen Oracle.

Retention has a named purpose, minimum justified period, scheduled expiry and verification across local/server/cache/log/export/backup stores. Security auditability is minimal metadata, not an unlimited exception to erasure or an excuse to retain raw health values. Keep sensitive data out of crash/support attachments. Access is least privilege, periodically reviewed and traceable without exposing payloads; third-party SDK data flows and subprocessors enter the inventory before use.

## Required later decisions and evidence

Before live use, legal/DPO resolves sensitive-data bases, special data-subject handling, notices/consent wording and proof, automated-decision transparency/review obligations, retention/exceptions, processor contracts, international transfers, rights response procedures and incident notification obligations. Decide whether an impact assessment or designated role is required in the actual operation. This foundation supplies no compliance verdict, legal deadline or presumed exemption.

Engineering/Security then verifies synthetic end-to-end permission/revocation, correction, export, deletion, offline retry, processor cleanup and restore/tombstone scenarios. Product/UX reviews comprehension, voluntariness and honest failure states before release; no Figma changes are part of this task. Store named decisions/evidence with the project and update this inventory rather than creating global product memory.
