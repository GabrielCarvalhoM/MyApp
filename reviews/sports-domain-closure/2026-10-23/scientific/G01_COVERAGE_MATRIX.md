# G01 coverage matrix
**SDC-SCI-r1 · AUTHOR_SUBMISSION · SSCTR-1.0.0 · 2026-10-23.**

All athlete states and doses are explicitly synthetic. Independent review, not this author, determines item approval. Foundation 0.2 remains candidate; runtime/Figma/clinical clearance are outside scope.

The internally reviewable subset covers regular, task-competent adults in five modes. It is an explicit review recipe library, not proof an executable product catalog exists. Beginner, special-population, specialized performance and clinical coverage are not inferred.

Each row supplies an actual week, recipe-specific equipment requirements, positive witness and four negative branches in the JSON. Missing a required item blocks that recipe; an explicitly reviewed alternative can remain available. Running skill does not establish lifting skill, and the converse also holds.

Required inputs:

- age/adult scope and materially relevant population context
- current restriction/symptom/illness/injury/clearance dependency answer, with provenance
- pause reason if returning
- current goals and phase; modality priority or co-priorities
- actual modality-specific recent exposure/chronology and comparable response
- configuration-specific competence; support/coaching gap
- recipe-specific equipment availability
- current recovery/context sufficient for intended decision
- running effort/intensity method and freshness/context if needed
- strength load conventions, RIR uncertainty and task technique if needed
- complete affected schedule, planned/executed status, commitments/change cost
- reconciled facts/revision/provenance and explicit agency

Clinical concern means persistent pain, injury/illness-related return, pathology, rehab or clearance dependency. Refer to the qualified clinical human; no lower dose replaces that review. Unknown eligibility alone means ASK/HOLD on the dependent prescription, not a diagnosis or blanket referral.
