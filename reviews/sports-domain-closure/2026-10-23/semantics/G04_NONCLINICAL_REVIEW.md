# G04 — nonclinical boundaries and return

**Revision SDC-G04-NC-r1 · 2026-10-23 America/Sao_Paulo · AUTHOR_SUBMISSION.** Author: `/root/semantics_author`, Product/UX + Data semantics workstream. This is a proposed routing/boundary contract and synthetic review submission; an independent SSCTR sporting judge must review it. No clinical judgment, medical clearance, human validation, new dose, Product Truth promotion, runtime implementation or Figma change is issued.

## Bound question and authority

For the reviewed adult recreational V1 subset, when can a documented agenda/travel interruption or nonclinical constraint enter an applicable training-policy review, when is a minimum question/hold necessary, and when is the question outside the agent's authority? Current SSCTR-1.0.0 §§2/5/6, SSCTR-EP-1.0.0, PDM-1.3 §5, AEOS HC02/03/07 + PS04, SIS §6 and RIL §5 are the baseline. Founder explicitly retained clinical human review and authorized the current nonclinical closure round.

The target separates **unknown eligibility**, **nonclinical training uncertainty**, **unavailable recipe/authority**, and **clinical dependency**. A generic elapsed-time return rule was rejected because it would manufacture coverage and individual readiness. Automatically restoring the former plan was rejected because interruption context and current feasibility may differ. The chosen path uses current documented context plus an independently approved applicable re-entry recipe, with no replacement of missed work.

The selected synthetic ages, pause lengths, sets, minutes and dates in companion witnesses identify cases only. They do not set universal eligibility, detraining, progression, recovery or return thresholds. A reported absence of clinical concerns is an input within the nonclinical fixture, not an assessment that no clinical risk exists.

## Required return facts and state

Record the interruption reason as declared, start/end and what was actually performed; former plan/target/fact revisions and previous comparable response; current objective/priority/phase and event if any; availability/equipment/configuration/competence; current eligibility information and restrictions/holds; current recovery/context and uncertainty; applicable re-entry recipe/rule/authority revision; planned horizon and next recheck. Ask only decision-critical missing inputs. No pause reason may be guessed from a silent calendar or missing sync.

`INTERRUPTED_DECLARED → RETURNING` describes continuity, not cleared physiology or automatically resumed training. Former volume/pace/load remains history. No missed session becomes debt, a makeup workout, a restored streak, earned progression or evidence of detraining. Recipe absence retains the dependent prescription; independent recording/goal/context clarification can proceed.

These are internal boundary/routing rules, not a clinical screening, red-flag urgency or emergency-copy protocol. They add no diagnostic or medical thresholds. Clinical holds cannot be released by user acceptance, subscription, friendly score, elapsed time, absence of pain alone, unrelated wearable metric or agent vote. A user's correction or disagreement is recorded and routed to the competent owner; it does not silently erase a restriction.

## Concrete nonclinical paths and limits

The witnesses `G04-W01–W10` compare explicit nine-day travel and twenty-eight-day agenda pauses, unknown reason, stale eligibility after a relevant change, missing recipe, logistical partial replan, an isolated nonclinical fatigue signal, converging training-context signals, and separate persistent-pain/illness branches. The lengths are synthetic case facts with no duration-to-dose mapping. Every path states preserve/modify, agency and re-review; eligible alternatives and doses are gated by G01/G02 review.

For the travel-positive boundary case, actual activity during travel is known, current context is explicitly supplied, prior run/strength prescriptions are historical comparison facts and no clinical dependency is given. The expected result is **eligibility for a bounded nonclinical recipe review**, not an instruction to repeat the historical 37-minute run or 3×5 strength task. In the twenty-eight-day case a new work schedule removes two weekly slots, creating a concrete logistical need to reconsider the horizon; prescription and level-5 sporting sufficiency remain dependent on the separately reviewed re-entry candidate. If no such candidate is approved, `HOLD` persists.

No exercise alternatives, nominally easy run, load reduction, “start gradually” dose or general return progression is improvised to bridge missing recipe authority. This author deliberately leaves that physiological approval to the independent SSCTR review of concrete G01/G02 recipes. An approval of these boundaries must not be counted as closure of an absent re-entry dose.
