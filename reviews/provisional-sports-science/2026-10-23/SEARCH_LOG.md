## Selection, access and verification

Selected: recognized position/consensus statements and peer-reviewed systematic reviews/meta-analyses directly relevant to healthy adult RT/CT, acute running responses and intensity measurement; one measured-threshold original study and one 2026 acute crossover as auxiliary evidence. Title, DOI and population/endpoint were checked against PubMed or the actual publisher. Full-text access is declared source by source in [snapshot](LITERATURE_SNAPSHOT.json). A PubMed abstract is a verified abstract, not a verified full paper. No abstract-only source independently approves a prescription.

Material access limitations: PubMed sometimes returned only an HHS footer; adding `?format=pubmed` did not solve it. Relevant publisher pages or indexed PubMed text were then used. PMC sometimes returned a browser challenge; no challenge bypass was attempted. Held 2026 publisher explicitly exposed a subscription preview. ACSM 2026 PubMed indexed abstract and PMC indexed selected sections were readable, but the full PMC page challenged and DOI redirected to inaccessible Ovid. Campos DOI/Thieme, Cowley DOI/Taylor & Francis and Riebe publisher full text were inaccessible. The initially attempted acute-review URL `10.1186/s40798-022-00506-4` was **not** a verified reference, failed retrieval and was discarded; PubMed verified the correct Silva DOI `10.1186/s40798-022-00497-w`, then its publisher text was read.

Corrections/retraction check: inspected available source/PubMed/publisher records for notices, plus queries 7/10/11/14/15. No notice was observed for the nine admitted references **on the retrieved records**. This is not an exhaustive Crossmark/Retraction Watch audit. Zhao et al. 2026, PMID42732577, DOI10.1080/02640414.2026.2732611, was discovered as a newer CT/endurance review; its PubMed record links **Correction PMID42788310 / DOI10.1080/02640414.2026.2739694**. The correction body was not accessible. It is excluded from substantive inference pending inspection, rather than counted as independent confirmation. The current search still establishes that Held's February-2025 search cutoff is not a complete 2026 evidence snapshot.

Other exclusions: 2026 semi-systematic sequencing review (less direct/rigorous than included systematic syntheses; no added decision-changing information); elite-athlete review PMID42866183 (outside accepted recreational pilot population; found, not used to approve elite scope); flywheel/sprint and short-bout-HIIT reviews (different intervention/endpoint); blogs, news summaries, social, commercial trainers, preprint BFR clinical material, unrelated disease reviews and third-party PDF mirrors (not evidentiary bases). No evidence from a manufacturer's claim or forum.

Stop rationale: accessible literature can bound interpretation and support a conservative candidate definition; it cannot supply the missing athlete eligibility, goals, intensity calibration, response history, or availability. Additional general papers will not make S01/S02 individually evaluable. Specific missing proofs and next owners are recorded in the pilot/backlog; no administrative human-availability blocker is carried forward for nonclinical scientific review.
