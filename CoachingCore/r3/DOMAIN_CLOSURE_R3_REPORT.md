## What changed

- A pure adapter retains every source fact with explicit unknown/absent/N/A/provenance and a deterministic trace. Typed sessions retain per-hand versus total loads, per-set vectors, partial completed work and local day/windows. No age, timezone, date, wearable requirement, capacity, physiological zone or score is invented.
- Canonical schema validation is separate from material consumption contracts. Empty/untyped/malformed request operands produce a software-contract ASK, never useful ASK. Actual missing/unknown coaching facts produce substantive ASK. Supplying those facts transitions to the actual remaining domain assessment rather than repeating a false missing claim.
- Full projection/provenance/diagnostic integrity is checked. Conflicting sources, duplicate/stale observations, unreviewed current context, interruption and explicit clinical observations fail closed. Stable input is deterministic; official and completed state remain unchanged.
- After the pre-expansion baseline, exactly two candidate task recipes were considered. Independent SSCTR approved the exact familiar steady10/15/5min at355s/km, with personal effort cue and no physiological-zone claim. The familiar4x15s strides block was ABSTAIN/ASK because preparation/full-session context is missing. No warm-up or extra easy minutes were added to inflate coverage. The existing20min easy witness reuses the unchanged R2-P14 review through canonical source association.
- The single future funded-human queue preserves its100 original entries and adds only the actual steady approval. Funding remains NOT_DECLARED. No abstention is queued.

## What the measurement can and cannot say

Consumption means source-bound typed material operands support an eligibility, missing-information, exact-policy or explicit domain/science-gap assessment. It does not mean sports sufficiency, arbitrary prose comprehension, automatic athlete-state inference or a dose-ready case. Values left in narrative form remain narrative; known numeric source fields are not extrapolated. The schema can express measured/self-reported/inferred/learned origins; the implemented ingestion here is explicitly synthetic-only, not a production wearable ingestion system.

The original42 were independently authored before R2 evaluation. R3 authors saw and implemented against this challenge set; it is reused, not a fresh blinded holdout. New variants and84 multiweek rows are excluded from the42-case denominator. Case-level whole requests are required: a familiar run subtask does not satisfy an initial hybrid week. Subgroups overlap and must not be summed. [Facet coverage](TRUE_COVERAGE_BASELINE_R3.json) retains planning, run, strength, adaptation, recovery, return, taper and learning counts.

Three taper profiles remain separate: recreational10K Run-first, recreational half-marathon Run-first, and Balanced with temporary event priority. Known familiar components do not establish the combined week, baseline-relative reduction or maintenance efficacy. Their3 science-primary gaps also retain domain and substantive baseline/configuration blockers. Conversely35/36/40 are domain/current-task-binding gaps with unsupported-crosswalk limitations, not falsely labelled as a demand for more literature.
