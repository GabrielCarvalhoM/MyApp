## Inclusion, overlap and negative findings

Only peer-reviewed papers/recognized scientific position stands and official publisher/PubMed/NCBI records support scientific claims. Commercial blogs, social posts, Reddit, aggregators, manufacturer claims, clinical-return studies outside scope and preprints were not policy authorities. Institutional author copies are distinguished from publisher records. Source hierarchy was assessed by applicability, not vote count.

S01 protocol and S02 outcome are one trial. S03 and S04 overlap in evidence; they are not independent replications. The2026 concurrent umbrella also overlaps older meta-analyses. No trial directly establishing the exact hybrid1x5+10K/HM week was identified in this bounded search: C09 remains INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. This absence is not proof no future/equivalent study exists. No literature establishes the exact universal set/repetition changes, count of athlete episodes,30min walking criterion, seven-day taper optimum or chronological return percentage; those cannot be represented as scientific laws.
