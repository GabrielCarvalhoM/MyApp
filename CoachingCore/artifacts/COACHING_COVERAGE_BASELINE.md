# Coaching coverage baseline

The original24 ACF scenarios produce 6 concrete positive proposal decisions; the remaining classes are reported separately. This is a designed-case count, not V1 population coverage.

| ACF24 class | Count |
|---|---:|
| POSITIVE_PRESCRIPTION_OR_ADAPTATION_SUPPORTED | 6 |
| SUPPORTED_KEEP | 10 |
| USEFUL_ASK | 6 |
| CORRECT_ABSTAIN_OR_HOLD | 2 |
| NOT_SUPPORTED_YET | 0 |
| CLINICAL_OR_OUT_OF_SCOPE | 0 |

KEEP, ASK, HOLD and ABSTAIN never enter the positive numerator. Exact accepted withdrawal branches are SUPPORTED_KEEP. SDC successors, novice counterpart, daily no-ops, failure probes and replay have separate denominators. Full positive V1 coverage remains NOT_READY.
