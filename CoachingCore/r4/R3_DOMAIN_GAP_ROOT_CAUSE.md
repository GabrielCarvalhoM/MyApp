# R3 domain gaps: root-cause compression

16 unchanged cases reduce to five reusable capability clusters. A solved domain classification is not automatically a positive: missing material calibration may correctly become ASK.

## ESTABLISHMENT

Cases: REP-R2-01, REP-R2-02, REP-R2-03, REP-R2-06, REP-R2-07.

Root: Treating lack of imported/product history or exact complete recipe witness as inability to compose productive entry.

Capability: R4-ESTABLISHMENT. Model inputs: reported recent exposure, movement competence, goal, availability, equipment, response. Science: Sufficient to bound low-demand entry; not whole-program efficacy or unknown loaded competence. Review: Independent envelope and binding review. Consumers: first run/strength session, first integrated week.

## MINIMUM_EFFECTIVE_ADAPTATION

Cases: REP-R2-09, REP-R2-10, REP-R2-11, REP-R2-12.

Root: No reusable selection between KEEP, optional omission, prospective suffix termination and earned familiar progression/regression.

Capability: R4-LOCAL-FATIGUE, R4-SYSTEMIC-SUFFIX, R4-STRENGTH-ADAPTATION. Model inputs: actual per-set/per-repetition work, comparable response, essential/optional role, current local/systemic context. Science: Sufficient to bound task/effort continuity and avoid unearned work; exact operation is coaching inference. Review: Independent envelopes and prospective binding. Consumers: in-session remaining work, next strength prescription.

## FEASIBLE_PRIORITY_SCHEDULING

Cases: REP-R2-15, REP-R2-16.

Root: Priority known but no selector comparing feasible familiar same-dose rearrangements.

Capability: R4-SCHEDULE. Model inputs: available windows, familiar configurations, key/maintenance roles, priority, next key work. Science: Sufficient for conditional preference; no universal spacing or causal interference rule. Review: Envelope plus window/duration binding. Consumers: hybrid conflict/double placement.

## CALIBRATED_RUN_OPTIONS

Cases: REP-R2-35, REP-R2-36, REP-R2-37, REP-R2-40.

Root: One exact-dose gate conflated earned familiar progression and missing current task calibration; no alternative/establishment path.

Capability: R4-FAMILIAR-RUN, R4-ESTABLISHMENT. Model inputs: recent comparable run exposure, personal effort anchor, candidate tolerance, event and environment. Science: Sufficient for familiar long progression; proprietary VO2/flat-to-fast/old-race crosswalk remains unjustified. Review: Envelope plus preserving targeted ASK when material anchors absent. Consumers: long run next step, VO2/short interval/race intent eligibility.

## INDIVIDUAL_OPTION_PREFERENCE

Cases: REP-R2-41.

Root: No association-aware ranking of independently admissible same-dose options.

Capability: R4-OBSERVATIONAL-RANKING, R4-SCHEDULE. Model inputs: consent, observational comparability, recency, consistent comparable responses, feasibility. Science: Sufficient for soft reversible preference; symbolic options alone do not prove schedule feasibility. Review: Envelope and no-causality binding. Consumers: hybrid option ranking, athlete model.
