# Bounded coaching decision model

Offline synthetic Node24 reference; best-supported among generated admissible candidates, never universal physiological optimality.

1. What primary/secondary goal and horizon are supplied?
2. What phase/block is supplied, unknown or inferred as request intent?
3. What essential stimulus must survive?
4. What comparable exposure and actual response are known?
5. Which envelopes and concrete options are admissible under current approval?
6. Which valid option best serves this goal and phase?
7. What secondary trade-off is accepted?
8. What smallest sufficient change addresses the material observation?
9. What uncertainty remains without invented facts?
10. What observation will retain, progress, regress or refute this choice?

The selector first rejects inadmissible options, then applies context-specific ordinal preferences. KEEP is a real candidate, with a reason to preserve or reject it. A conflict requires feasible alternatives; priority alone cannot prove that an arrangement works. An available familiar progression can outrank KEEP when earned, while an isolated noisy deviation normally cannot. No universal arithmetic utility score hides these choices.

Each material positive trace must identify the goal, phase (including unknown), intended stimulus, all generated alternatives, why valid/invalid/suboptimal, selected option, preference, preserved work, trade-off, uncertainty and next observation. A missing explicit goal is recorded as a limitation; request intent must never be relabelled a known athlete goal. Generalization is bounded by implemented request vocabulary.

Quality is independent of the action label. A MODIFY can be suboptimal; KEEP or a targeted ASK can be best-supported. Runtime self-assessment cannot serve as the independent result.
