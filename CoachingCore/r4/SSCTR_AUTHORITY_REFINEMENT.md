# SSCTR authority refinement R4

Direct Founder instruction 2026-10-25 corrects the overly literal exact-output review interpretation for this offline mission. SSCTR remains provisional internal scientific authority under SSCTR-1.0.0; it reviews principles, populations, admissible envelopes, progression/regression plausibility, scientific extrapolations, contraindications and uncertainty. Coaching Intelligence owns a traceable concrete choice inside that reviewed space.

A paper need not prove each generated set, minute, repetition, load or local-time arrangement. A 3×8 selection may be defensible within reviewed controlled-hard exposure/progression constraints; the choice still needs relevant personal anchors, current context, alternative comparison and response review. A smallest available strength increment is an equipment-constrained coaching choice, not a new scientific law.

The prior protocol's prohibition on invented numeric precision remains: unsupported physiological thresholds, crosswalks and causal claims are prohibited. It does not prohibit explicit reversible operational choices inside a defensible envelope. Population principles support the option space; the selected operational value does not become universal, optimal or empirically individualized merely because it is used. SSCTR may reject the envelope or its implementation binding.

Authority split:

| Owner | Decision | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| SSCTR | Principle/envelope scientific defensibility and conditions | No runtime sovereignty, own-target approval, clinical clearance or efficacy claim |
| Coaching Intelligence | Candidate composition, ordinal ranking, concrete choice and trace | Must stay within approved hash-bound constraints |
| Run/Strength/Hybrid consumer | Executable session/remaining-work/schedule | Must preserve dose basis, intent and completed work |
| Athlete Model | Observations, comparable history, soft preference and provenance | Association is not causation or restored capacity |
| Independent quality judge | Was a better admissible option ignored? Was MEC/goal/phase respected? | Does not confer empirical validation |
| Founder/future qualified human | Reserved scope/strategy and funded professional revalidation | AI cannot veto human modification/revocation |

One current independent review activates reusable envelope content; generated concrete instances do not each need a new review or queue item. Out-of-envelope changes or materially new scientific rules invalidate/reopen the relevant review. Prior finite approvals remain history and their earlier runtime versions retain their semantics. Joint taper, novel pace crosswalks and clinical scope are not automatically closed by this refinement.
