# Four-horizon reasoning — R5

Every material generation/review records H0–H3 and validates the complete candidate before a proposal. A missed exposure creates no training debt. Event week and completed sessions cannot be overwritten by ordinary progression.

Scope: OFFLINE_SYNTHETIC_NODE24_REFERENCE. Candidate implementation; not Product Truth, production, clinical or empirical validation.

## horizons

- H0_SESSION: Complete executable dose; Actual source and current task response; Feasible window and equipment
- H1_WEEK: Preserve protected run and strength stimuli; Complete combined tolerance; One prospective dose dimension per next horizon
- H2_BLOCK: Intent continuity; Repeated response versus isolated noise; Avoid unnecessary partial/block replanning
- H3_GOAL_EVENT: Goal contribution; Event date and separately bound event configuration; No compression or overload near event
