# Strength trajectories — R5

Familiar strength is an intrinsic objective in strength-first and balanced inputs. Complete exercises retain load basis, preparation, rest, reps, sets and RIR. Dose alternatives can vary reps, load, sets or RIR in one supported direction; no obligatory weight increase exists. Full actual exercise/load records are required for dose learning.
