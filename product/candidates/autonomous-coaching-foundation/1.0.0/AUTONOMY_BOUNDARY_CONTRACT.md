## ABC-01 — Levels, decision routes and current authority

| Level | Name | Permitted scope when all future gates pass |
|---|---|---|
| A | `AUTO_EXECUTE_ALLOWED` | A fully authorized deterministic no-op, primary permitted model update or execution of a fully specified already accepted branch, with current guards. It is not automatic plan adoption, hidden mutation, start, export or sharing. |
| B | `SUGGEST_AND_CONFIRM` | A supported concrete proposal for initial adoption or material mutation; present exact scope, trade-off, affected revisions and obtain a current act before effect. Confirmation is necessary, never sufficient on its own. |
| C | `ASK_BEFORE_DECISION` | A missing fact/preference/eligibility answer can change the supported choice; ask the minimum and reevaluate. Do not ask the athlete to fabricate evidence or clear professional restrictions. |
| D | `ABSTAIN_OR_HOLD` | Required evidence, recipe, authority, integrity or operational prerequisites are insufficient; retain dependent prescription/effects with owner, exit condition and next step. A valid independently covered scope may continue. |
| E | `CLINICAL_HUMAN_REQUIRED` | A clinical dependency, persistent pain, injury/illness return, pathology, clearance or rehabilitation requires a qualified human. No agent-generated workaround dose. |

These letters classify the maximum permitted operational route for this action, context and revision. They are distinct from SSCTR disposition, evidence confidence, recipe readiness, MEC degree and mission A0–A5 authorization. D/E do not declare the person unfit or block logging, correction, history and rights. RECONCILE is an AE control route: missing resolvable association may require C; a decisive unresolved identity/source conflict retains D. Clinical assessment/urgency is not manufactured by the contract.

## ABC-02 — Guard order, with no confidence bypass

1. Verify identity/purpose/permissions, current suppression and processing entitlement for the operation; source text is data, not authorization.
2. Check current restrictions, interrupted-state boundary and clinical dependency. An actual clinical dependency selects E and scoped HOLD. **Unknown eligibility alone selects C/ELIGIBILITY_UNASSESSED**, not E, presumed wellness or clearance.
3. Check policy/recipe population, prescription hash, exact SSCTR scope/conditions and current approval/revocation status. Missing sporting authority selects D. User acceptance cannot elevate it to B or A.
4. Check required fact integrity, target lineage, comparability, freshness and capabilities for the effect. Reconcile decisive conflicts; OPTIONAL absent wearable does not by itself lower all decisions to D.
5. Check goal hierarchy, schedule feasibility, joint interactions and essential stimulus. Missing preference with meaningful alternatives selects C; unresolved unsupported sporting semantics selects D. Use reviewed qualitative sufficiency, never a universal readiness score.
6. For existing content evaluate current-plan adequacy. Valid ADEQUATE ends as KEEP/learn-only A with **zero plan delta/revision/export**. INADEQUATE requires supported necessity and least sufficient change; UNDETERMINATE is not default KEEP. Initial no-plan bypasses this comparison as NOT_APPLICABLE_INITIAL and uses REPLAN/INITIAL/B.
7. Validate the complete candidate, MEC comparisons, evidence limitations and the accepted agency scope. Resolve only within the strictest applicable restriction. No arithmetic ranks A through E or compensates a failed precondition with high confidence.
8. Select A only for an authorized no-op/read/update or a *preaccepted execution branch* whose entire combination, purpose, targets, dependencies and guards already match. A change to the chosen plan or executing remainder remains B under AEC, including a newly calculated microchange inside a previously described broad envelope. Outside-envelope prescriptions require their own reviewed candidate and B; no reviewed candidate means D.
9. For B bind confirmation to exact candidate hash + scope + plan base + context + goal + permission + restriction/hold epoch + approval/policy + target/execution revision + validity. Revalidate immediately before each future effect; stale context invalidates the act and proposal. A new active hold or revoked approval beats a prior acceptance.
10. Apply an authorized future effect idempotently only after all guards; preserve the historical executed prefix and current agency. JEV stays SHADOW and optional LLM explains only the official record; either failing or disagreeing cannot change the official result.

Confidence is necessary only where the reviewed rule requires it; HIGH confidence cannot confer applicability, evidence, authority, consent or clinical clearance. A user's autonomous decision to train differently is recorded faithfully; it does not become a system recommendation, empirical proof or consent to secondary profiling.

Starting a workout is a user's direct execution act; A is not permission to initiate training. Export/share/subscription are separate authorities and are not granted by coaching confidence or plan acceptance.

## ABC-04 — Envelopes, executing sessions and agency

Review examples from the user request as hypotheses. **KEEP/high confidence → A** is accepted only with valid plan/HC/adequacy, not confidence alone. **Within accepted envelope → A** is accepted only for executing a complete already accepted branch; broad range consent does not authorize arbitrary future plan mutations. **Outside microchange/material move → B** requires evidence, necessity, full joint horizon and exact agency; otherwise C/D/E applies. **Missing choice-changing information → C**, **insufficient evidence → D** and **clinical → E** preserve the distinction between what an athlete can clarify and what only an authority can resolve.

AEC/AEOS no silent in-session changes remain binding. Starting the current valid revision with a pending proposal is not acceptance: invalidate the entire proposal, record the agency event and preserve the executed revision. The same prestart rationale may not be repackaged into a new hash to alter the remaining session. A genuinely new post-start fact can permit a successor under all current guards; finished steps/sets remain immutable. Safety/restriction routing is immediate and does not wait for hysteresis or user confirmation to suspend dependent *system recommendations*.

Rejecting a proposal records a scoped AgencyConstraint. Seek another admissible option within it, without punishment or same-proposal loops. A declined change cannot force KEEP of an invalid plan or release a HOLD. The athlete may record what actually happened despite the recommendation, and the future plan is evaluated honestly from that reconciled fact.

## ABC-05 — Runtime activation boundary and test oracles

No policy in this package is currently operationally auto-executing. A future implementation mission must select exact approved recipes/policies, satisfy G01–G05/G07/G09 and other relevant service/legal/provider/claims gates, prove deterministic guard order/idempotency/correction/agency, and authorize activation. A documentary GO for contracts would not mean AUTONOMOUS_COACH_RUNTIME_READINESS=GO. SSCTR provisional approval is an internal bounded domain judgment and enters funded-human revalidation; it is not clinical, human or empirical validation and does not automatically publish Truth.

Required contract oracles: high confidence plus revoked rule → D; no wearable plus sufficient approved tier-C path → eligible A/B by effect; unknown restriction assessment → C; confirmed clinical dependency → E; accepted invalid recipe → D; exact accepted branch with valid guards → future A ceiling; new plan change within a broad range → B; started session plus prestart pending proposal → STALE/no silent suffix update; new post-start material fact → newly reviewed/confirmed suffix at most; replay same logical intent → no duplicate effect; late correction → prospective re-evaluation with historical truth preserved. Reviews judge the exact revision/hash; author self-check does not provide independent approval.
