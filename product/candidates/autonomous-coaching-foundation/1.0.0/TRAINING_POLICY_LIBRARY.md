## POL-01 — progression

Choose a justified next-horizon increment while an adequate current week remains stable.

População: Adult recreational nonclinical V1; modality-specific eligibility, competence, recipe familiarity and current context are required. A novice route is separate, never inferred from the other sport.

Entradas: Comparable executions and executed-target revisions; Configuration-specific capability and effort anchors; Goal, phase, schedule, other-modality exposure; Recipe next-step candidates and response sufficiency.

| Ação | Condição | Efeito | Autonomia / MEC |
|---|---|---|---|
| P01-K: KEEP | Current week remains adequate. | Learn and prepare next horizon without mutating this week. | A / 0 |
| P01-N: REPLAN | Repeated comparable success and bounded next step admissible; next horizon. | Select one prelisted next-step dose; reject simultaneous load, volume and density escalation. | B / 1 |

Preservar: Current chosen week; Essential stimulus and technique; Unchanged dimensions in selected next step. Trade-offs: Potential gain versus comparison noise and disruption; Smallest available equipment increment can be too large.

Atalhos proibidos: Calendar-driven increase; 10% rule as universal gate; One PR proves adaptation; Percent1RM/RIR default without calibration.

Fallback: Repeat familiar dose, or ASK for missing comparable evidence; HOLD dose outside reviewed recipe.
