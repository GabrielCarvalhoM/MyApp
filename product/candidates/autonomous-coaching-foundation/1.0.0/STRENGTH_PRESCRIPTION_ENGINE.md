## Inputs, output and task identity

Inputs: intrinsic strength goal; hybrid priority; strength-specific training age; current task competence by a stated method; equipment/increments and load convention; recent comparable performance, actual effort/rest/ROM; actual local/systemic exposure; planned running horizon; block phase; response history; eligibility; and accepted authority/envelope. Running proficiency is not strength competence. Equipment presence does not prove ability to use it. No body-mass, age, sex or running-pace formula supplies initial kilograms.

Output is a proposal/ASK/HOLD with exact recipe and exercise configuration revisions, ordered main/accessory tasks, work and warmup sets, repetitions per side/total, external load and units/convention, estimated RIR or approved effort method and timing, rest/advance, ROM/tempo when material, demand interpretation and confidence, intended weekly frequency/volume relationship, progression/substitution rules, source/decision refs, scope of accepted adaptation and next check. The specialist never writes a parallel plan; Orchestrator validates both modalities and emits the single current plan under AEC.

Identity includes variant, implements/machine setup, stance/grip when material, assistance, load convention, ROM and side. A machine label or kg-per-hand is not interchangeable with total barbell mass. Actual physical sets are canonical facts; local muscle/pattern attributions are interpretations that cannot multiply systemic workload. Primary/secondary muscle set attributions do not establish fractional effective sets or equivalent tonnage across tasks. Estimated 1RM is omitted unless an authorized model applies; no mandatory max test is introduced.

## Demand construction

**Exercise metadata × prescription × athlete/context → estimated training demand.** Metadata contains movement, primary/secondary regions, bilateral/unilateral convention, stability, ROM, resistance setup, technical complexity and contextual axial/eccentric descriptors. Prescription adds sets/reps/load, proximity-to-failure estimate, rests/density, ordering and total exposure. Athlete/context adds task familiarity, demonstrated competence, actual recent exposure, response history, current local/systemic state, running schedule and goal.

The result contains: physical dose; local group/pattern attribution; expected local/systemic burden with rationale; novelty; comparison to relevant familiar tasks; measurement limits; confidence; next check; and unresolved questions. There is **no universal exercise fatigue score** and no validated multiplication coefficient. A squat does not have fixed fatigue points. Short accessories may add meaningful local demand, and a set reduction is not guaranteed to preserve the original adaptation or restore tomorrow's readiness. Shared exposure references prevent double counting.

## Establishing load and novice route

For familiar tasks, use a comparable known load/reps/reserve configuration and current context; choose the finite dose that meets the accepted goal. A change of machine, implement, ROM or assistance can invalidate load comparability and requires its own baseline. Never transfer barbell load or PR proportionally to dumbbells just because both are a squat.

For a strength novice, establish task competence through a bounded approved instruction/familiarization method and ask the minimum material equipment/history/eligibility questions. Introduce familiarizable tasks with athlete-controlled submaximal attempts, record actual reps/task control and explicit uncertainty in estimated reserve, and stop/reassess on deterioration. The goblet/push/pull/hinge examples show how an established synthetic initial load would be represented; 8 kg is not the default starting load for real beginners. If there is no supported task/load establishment route, HOLD that task and retain independently eligible known tasks. A new exercise is not made safe by naming it novice.

STR-NOVICE-A and STR-NOVICE-B are finite familiarization candidates with separate modality eligibility and no near-failure requirement. Subsequent repetition increases are conditional options after repeated comparable competent response, not a fixed novice calendar. The exact initial load and broad zero-history autonomous competence assessment remain explicit gaps for independent review. No video/movement assessment capability is presumed to exist.

## Strength, hypertrophy support, maintenance and running support

Strength emphasis protects task-specific main exposure and deliberate load progression, rather than treating it as accessory work for running. Hypertrophy-supportive programming reviews aggregate weekly muscle/pattern exposure and goal-specific volume; this one full-body session does not certify a whole hypertrophy block. Maintenance deliberately retains selected familiar exposures under its accepted goal and response trend; reduced volume is not proven equivalent. Running support has an explicit lower/plantar-flexor purpose without promising injury prevention, rehabilitation or economy improvement for the individual. Balanced planning cannot silently demote strength whenever running is on the same day.

Healthy-adult RT evidence supports progressive, goal-specific resistance training with several workable configurations and does not make failure mandatory. It does not determine an exact optimum for these synthetic composite sessions, novice loads or an individual's adaptation. [ACSM Position Stand 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12965823/). This is broad-principle support; exact bounded program approval still requires independent SSCTR applicability review.

## Progression and autoregulation

Each exercise carries a named next candidate: for the familiar lower witness, high-bar squat 60 -> 62.5 kg with the same 3 x 5, while RDL/accessories are held; for upper, bench 40 -> 42.5 kg is analogous. These are equipment-representable design options, not mandatory increments or inferred scientific cutoffs. A named hypertrophy-supportive next repetition option allows one exercise's reps to advance before load while sets/rest remain stable; a new rep target is not already proven by the original 5-rep history. No minimum number of sessions, fixed weekly percentage, or automatic increase at the top of a rep range is asserted as universal physiology.

Earned progression requires repeated comparable performance with actual task control, reported reserve/rest and acceptable subsequent response, relevant contrary evidence, goal and hybrid horizon. The sufficiency rationale names which observations are comparable and why the pattern is stable; a single PR or completed set is insufficient by itself. Unknown RIR is unknown, not RIR 2. Deliberately reduced work earns adherence to the revised dose; it does not earn progression of omitted original work. An unchanged load can be correct programming.

Autoregulation is a conditional measurement-and-adjustment process. RIR is an estimate with task/training-age error; set RPE, session RPE and pain remain distinct. Reps at a percentage of 1RM do not guarantee a known reserve. Autoregulation research is method/population bound and does not justify a universal readiness-to-load equation. [Hickmott et al., autoregulation review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40798-021-00404-9). A nonsignificant comparison is not equivalence proof. These recipes do not import velocity-loss thresholds or require velocity hardware.

Overperformance: record actual reserve/reps/load under the current target and update future opportunity. Do not append sets today or chase failure. Underperformance: first reconcile units, configuration, target revision, rest, technique-report validity and context. One hard set is not systemic fatigue or evidence that running caused interference. If the accepted remainder is materially inadequate, evaluate the smallest sufficient named alternative. Preserve current adequate work when a transient observation does not warrant change.

## Deload, missed sessions and substitution

Deload reduces/redistributes demand when the actual cycle/context supports it; no fixed week or universal percentage applies. Candidate reduction can remove a named last set, reduce an explicitly established familiar load, or select STR-MAINTENANCE when the revised intent is adequate. Which change is sufficient depends on the current task and full horizon; “smaller” alone does not prove adequacy. Taper is event-directed and jointly reviewed with Running, not the same rule as deload. Complete combined taper and nonclinical return trajectories are not approved merely by having a reduced session recipe.

For a missed session, confirm absence versus delayed sync; protect the adequate remainder. No automatic doubling, extra failure sets, volume debt or mandatory next-day placement. Reschedule only if the stimulus remains necessary and the whole next-session/week horizon fits, with material move confirmation. If not rescheduled, mark progression opportunity NOT_EVALUATED and continue the adequate accepted plan.

Exercise substitution compares intent, pattern/muscle involvement, skill, equipment, dose, local/systemic uncertainty and retained/lost specificity. STR-SUB-DB is a named equipment candidate only for known separate DB task loads. It preserves some pattern exposure and loses barbell-specific stimulus. Record a new configuration baseline without deleting the old history. Unknown skill/load asks or holds the dependent task; pain does not trigger a generic safe substitute catalog. Supersets/drop sets are outside this finite library and need their own member/segment/rest/counting/demand structure and review.

## In-session and authority contract

Completed sets, actual values and accepted target revisions are immutable. A bounded preaccepted option can alter only future sets under the current accepted envelope and runtime authority. An example is omitting the final optional accessory set when this is the smallest sufficient reviewed alternative; another is reducing a remaining main-work set where the essential goal remains adequate. They are not interchangeable. If not preaccepted, even a small outside-envelope change is a suggestion requiring current confirmation. A stopped set is an observed event, not consent to rewrite the week.

Unknown decision-changing eligibility/competence/load selects C / ASK and dependent HOLD. Unsupported exact dose selects D for that dependent prescription. Persistent pain, injury/illness, new functional limitation, pathology or clearance selects E / CLINICAL_HUMAN_REQUIRED; no deload or substitution serves as clinical workaround. Independent valid work may remain eligible only under its own known constraints. No autonomy level grants a physiologically unsupported action authority, and candidate documentation never grants production AUTO_EXECUTE permission.

Deterministic schema/arithmetic checks do not prove sporting validity, good technique, safety or outcome.
